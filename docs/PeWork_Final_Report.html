Executive Summary: PeWork investigates the detection of fraudulent product reviews using XGBoost models trained on Amazon data and evaluates cross-platform generalization to 358k Yelp restaurant reviews. We introduce strict causal point-in-time splits, empirical decile probability calibration, and a production Streamlit decision-support application with real-time text highlighting and what-if counterfactual sensitivity.

5. Empirical Decile Probability Calibration

Person A's audit uncovered severe miscalibration in uncalibrated raw scores: in the top score decile (mean predicted probability 99.6%), the empirical fraud rate was only 55.8%. To resolve this for production triage, we mapped raw model outputs through holdout deciles using monotonic interpolation:

RAW_CAL_POINTS = np.array([0.0, 0.015, 0.025, 0.033, 0.042, 0.053, 0.068, 0.095, 0.208, 0.875, 0.996, 1.0])
OBS_CAL_POINTS = np.array([0.03, 0.0389, 0.0379, 0.0438, 0.0484, 0.0631, 0.0817, 0.1107, 0.1969, 0.8378, 0.5580, 0.60])

def calibrate_score(raw_score):
    return float(np.interp(np.clip(raw_score, 0.0, 1.0), RAW_CAL_POINTS, OBS_CAL_POINTS))

6. Cross-Platform Transfer Dynamics (Amazon vs Yelp)

Why Average Precision Drops from 0.822 to 0.122:
  1. Target Definition Discrepancy: Amazon ground truth captures seller manipulation rings and platform deletions. Yelp ground truth captures Yelp's recommendation filter, which flags unestablished accounts rather than solely malicious content.
  2. Absence of Cross-Platform User History: Top Amazon models rely on prior reviewer velocity (PR, NR). User identities do not link across platforms, forcing zero-history cold start predictions.
  3. Domain Shift: Consumer electronics and home goods emphasize packaging and build quality; restaurant reviews focus on hospitality, flavor, and wait times.

7. Production Streamlit Decision Support System

  • Interactive Phrase Highlighter: Identifies and color-codes purchase declarations (red), promo keywords (amber), slang (purple), and character repetitions (blue) in real-time.
  • 3-Tier Triage Framework: Low Risk (<20%, Automated Approval), Moderate Risk (20–37%, Watchlist), and High Risk (≥37%, Manual Inspection Required).
  • What-If Star-Rating Simulator: Evaluates the same review across 1★ through 5★ with an Altair sensitivity bar chart and threshold cutoff line.
  • CSV Batch Triage & Enriched Export: Processes up to 5,000 reviews and outputs an enriched CSV with Calibrated Risk, Risk Tier, and Top Driving SHAP Factors.

8. Phase 2 Research Roadmap (Jan–Feb 2027)

  • Competitor-Agenda & Coordinated Smear Detection: Deploying bipartite graph neural networks (GNNs) to identify coordinated negative review bombing and cross-competitor smear rings.
  • CNN Object-Level Image Reuse Detection: Deploying object-level feature extractors (YOLO/Faster-R-CNN) to detect cropped, mirrored, or watermarked product photos shared across fake accounts.
  • VLM Cross-Modal Consistency Checks: Implementing vision-language models (CLIP/PaliGemma) to flag contradictions where review text describes features absent in the attached photo.
